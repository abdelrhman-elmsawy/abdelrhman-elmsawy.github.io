SECURITY RESEARCH SCOUT

Turning security research into a source-grounded brief.

A practical cybersecurity research workflow designed to scan recent relevant research, surface the findings worth attention, and turn them into concise, reviewable briefs with explicit sources, uncertainty, and safe next steps.

01 / PROBLEM

Problem

Recent security research is valuable but noisy. A learner or practitioner can spend too much time searching, comparing sources, and deciding what actually matters. The challenge was to create a repeatable workflow that reduces that review burden without pretending that an AI-generated summary is authoritative.

02 / WHAT I DID

What I did

I designed Security Research Scout as a source-grounded workflow: define the security question, gather current research, select relevant sources, summarize only supported claims, surface uncertainty, and attach a safe next step that is explicitly limited to authorized environments.

ResearchSource checkingAI workflowSecurity
03 / WHAT CAME OF IT

What came of it

The result is a reusable research brief format that makes the evidence visible: source, date, finding, relevance, uncertainty, and safe action. It is designed as decision support rather than a replacement for human security analysis.

How it works

Question → current research → source selection → claim checking → concise findings → relevance and uncertainty → safe authorized next step → human review.

// guardrail

Honest limitation

The workflow can miss relevant sources or misunderstand complex technical material. Important claims and proposed actions still require human verification, and a research finding does not by itself prove that a system is vulnerable.
